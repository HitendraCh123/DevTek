Privacy Policy

At DevTek, protecting your privacy is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect the information you provide while visiting our website or using any of our professional services. Whether you contact us for Website Development, Mobile App Development, Digital Marketing, Cloud & Hosting Solutions, Branding, or Analytics, we are committed to ensuring your information remains safe and confidential.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when contacting us, requesting a quotation, submitting a support request, or filling out any form available on our website.

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Business Name
  • Website Address
  • Project Details
  • Company Information

We may also automatically collect technical information including your browser type, operating system, IP address, referring pages, device type, session duration, and pages visited.

2. How We Use Your Information

DevTek uses the collected information for legitimate business purposes including:

  • Providing requested IT services.
  • Preparing quotations and proposals.
  • Managing ongoing projects.
  • Responding to enquiries.
  • Sending invoices and project updates.
  • Improving our website performance.
  • Customer support.
  • Website security monitoring.
  • Marketing communication with your consent.
  • Legal and regulatory compliance.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience, understand visitor behaviour, remember preferences, and enhance website performance. Cookies may also be used for analytics, remarketing, and advertising purposes. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

4. Third Party Services

DevTek may integrate trusted third-party tools including:

  • Google Analytics
  • Google Ads
  • Google Search Console
  • Meta Pixel
  • Cloud Hosting Providers
  • Email Service Providers
  • Payment Gateways
  • CRM Software

These services have their own privacy policies and DevTek encourages users to review them.

5. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to safeguard your information, including secure servers, SSL encryption, access controls, firewalls, password protection, routine backups, and regular software updates. While we take every reasonable precaution, no internet-based transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Sharing Your Information

DevTek never sells, rents, or trades your personal information. We may only disclose information when:

  • Required by law.
  • Necessary to complete your requested service.
  • Working with trusted service providers.
  • Protecting our legal rights.
  • Preventing fraud or cyber threats.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to complete projects, fulfil legal obligations, resolve disputes, improve customer support, and maintain business records. When no longer required, information is securely deleted.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your applicable local laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw marketing consent.
  • Restrict certain processing activities.
  • Request data portability.

9. Children's Privacy

DevTek does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Our services are intended primarily for businesses and adults.

10. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Updated versions will always be published on this page with a revised Effective Date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, or wish to exercise any privacy rights, please contact us.
