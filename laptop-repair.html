Laptop Repair Services

Professional Screen Replacement, Battery Swaps, Keyboard Fixes, and Motherboard Diagnostics

Specialized Care

Expert Solutions for All Laptop Breakdowns

Laptops are built for mobility, which also makes them vulnerable to accidental drops, liquid spills, overheating, and battery wear. When your portable workstation starts failing, you need quick and precise technical support.

Our expert technicians specialize in component-level laptop repairs. From replacing cracked LCD/OLED screens and fixing broken charging ports to restoring water-damaged keyboards, we handle all major laptop makes and models with absolute precision.

HARDWARE & SOFTWARE

Laptop Problems We Solve

Broken or Flickering Screen

Immediate replacement of cracked displays, dead pixel panels, and malfunctioning ribbon cables for crisp visuals.

Dying Battery & Charging Issues

Swapping out swollen or exhausted batteries and repairing loose, bent, or broken internal DC charging jacks.

Liquid Spill Damage

Emergency ultrasonic chemical cleaning of motherboards and keyboard replacements to reverse coffee or water damage.

Overheating & Loud Fans

Clearing dust blockages, replacing clogged cooling fans, and applying high-grade thermal paste to lower temperatures.

Unresponsive Keyboards & Trackpads

Replacing stuck, missing, or water-damaged individual keys or the entire palm-rest assembly.

No Power / Won't Turn On

Advanced motherboard level diagnostics to track down short circuits, blown fuses, and power IC failures.
