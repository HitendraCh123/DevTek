Computer Repair & Upgrades

Expert Diagnostics, Hardware Repair, and Data Recovery for PC & Mac

Expert PC & Mac Repair

Is your computer running slow, crashing unexpectedly, or refusing to turn on altogether? Our certified technicians provide comprehensive diagnostics and repair services for all major brands of laptops and desktop computers.

From hardware failures like broken screens, faulty motherboards, and failing hard drives to software issues like the dreaded blue screen of death and malware infections, we have the expertise to get your system back up and running smoothly.

Hardware Upgrades & Data Recovery

Don't throw away a sluggish computer just yet! A simple RAM upgrade or switching from an old hard drive to a blazing-fast Solid State Drive (SSD) can make your old PC feel brand new again, saving you hundreds of dollars.

If you've experienced a sudden crash, liquid spill, or accidental deletion, our advanced data recovery tools can rescue your precious family photos, critical business documents, and creative projects from failing drives.
